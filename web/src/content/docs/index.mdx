## What it is

Tap along to a record and openBPM tells you its tempo, how far the pitch fader
has taken it from the label value, and how long the two decks will hold before
they drift apart. Built to be read at arm's length in a dark booth.

## Hardware

The [hardware notes](./docs/hardware/) cover the build: an ESP32, a display,
and the encoder and button wiring the firmware expects.
